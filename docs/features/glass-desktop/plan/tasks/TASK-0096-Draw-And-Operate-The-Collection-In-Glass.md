# Draw and operate the collection in Glass

## Definition of Done

- [x] The derived collection is readable in Glass's main rectangle and can be moved, resized and collapsed without a permanent navigator column. Shown by the smoke run's `collection` part: "the list is an object in the field and not a column beside it" (in the field true, a column beside the field false), the resize by its corner, the move by its header, and the collapse by Enter and by its ▾ button, each with the store read back. The `glass-desktop` walk shows the same three by pointer, and its picture `01-collection-on-the-field.png` shows the list with its rows readable.
- [x] Group folds, search, filters, counts, row opening and keyboard movement work in the object, including screen-reader names and reduced motion. A group heading: the `glass-collection` walk's check that a press on a heading folds its 10 rows away and says so to a screen reader, that Enter on it opens them again, and that the view's count does not change. Search: the smoke run's `collection` check where three letters narrow 144 notes to "59 of 144 notes", and the `glass-desktop` walk's check for a search with no match and Clear filters. The filters: the `glass-collection` walk chooses a status and then a type with the arrow keys, and each leaves the notes that match, what each holds and the notes that hold them ("10 of 144 notes" and "narrowed: status approved", then "narrowed: type requirement"). That is the list's own rule, `narrowGroups` in `desktop/src/shared/search.ts`. The count, a row opened by pointer and by Enter, and the arrow keys, Home and End: the smoke run's `keys` part, 12 checks. Reduced motion: "choosing a row highlights it", and the field cuts to it. The names and roles a screen reader is given are read from the page by the `glass-collection` walk: "Filter by status", "Filter by type", a list of list items, and a heading that says whether it is folded. Nobody has listened to them with a screen reader; that is for [[TST-0063-A-Collection-And-Full-Note-Share-Glass]].
- [x] A narrow window and the served page, which is what a tablet loads, keep a usable route to the same rows. Shown by the smoke run's `collection` checks in a field 652 px wide (a bar names the collection and each open note, "Collection" puts the list in front with the same count "144 notes", and the narrow window stores nothing), by the `served` part's check that a page with no bridge opens with the same exact count and that a row pressed there opens the note, and by the `glass-desktop` walk's five checks in a narrow field and three on the served page. On the served page the `glass-collection` walk also shows that a collection the Mac collapsed can be opened there: the page drew it 34 px tall with no row, its fold control opened it (76 rows on screen), folded it and opened it again, and in the Mac's window it stayed collapsed with the store's layout unchanged. The header's label there read "Features, 144 notes: Enter opens it". The served page was driven in a second Electron window with a mouse pointer, and its fold control was pressed by script. No real tablet and no touch was tried.

- [x] The collapsed header names the query, exact count and active filters; expanding restores its size, selection and scroll anchor. Shown by the `glass-collection` walk's check "collapsed, the header alone says the search, the count against the whole view and both filters; opened again the collection has its size, the row that was marked is marked, and the list is scrolled to the same row". Collapsed, the collection is 34 px tall and reads "Features", "10 of 144 notes" and "narrowed: “a” · status approved · type requirement". Opened again it is 340 by 693, FEAT-0020's row is marked and the list is 60 px down, all as before. With nothing narrowed, the smoke run's `collection` check on Enter and two checks of the `glass-desktop` walk show the header reading "Features" and "144 notes" and the list coming back to the same row.
- [x] Wheel input stays inside collection scrolling at both boundaries; dragging a header moves it while row text and controls keep their own behavior. Shown by the `glass-collection` walk's check "the wheel over the list at its first row, turned up, and at its last row, turned down, stays in the list: the field neither zooms nor turns" (the list stays at 0, then at 275 of 275 px, with the zoom and the bearing unchanged). The smoke run's `zoom` part shows the wheel over the collection scrolling its list (from 0 to 300) with the zoom left where it was, and a double-click on its header collapsing it without resetting the zoom. The header drag moves the collection and keeps its size, and letters typed in its search box are letters (`collection` and `zoom` parts).
- [x] Visible keyboard focus and the "collection" control reveal a covered or off-screen object; local Escape is consumed once. The control: "collection" is offered when the field is turned away and when a document lies over the list, brings the list back in front without turning the field, and puts the keyboard in it (three `collection` checks, and the `glass-desktop` walk's check on the served page). Choosing a row turns the field to that row's card (the `keys` checks under reduced motion), and Left, Right, End and Home on a row leave the collection in front where it stood. Escape: the `glass-collection` walk's check "Escape while the collection is being dragged ends the drag: it is back where it was, it stays there while the pointer moves on with the button still down and when the button is let go, the store is told nothing, and the key goes no further: the open note is still open". The collection was dragged from 192, 121 to 332, 132. It was at 192, 121 after Escape, after three more moves of the pointer and after the release, the store's layout was the same before and after, and FEAT-0001 was still open. The walk's check for the corner says the same of a resize: from 340 by 693 to 420 by 717, and 340 by 693 after Escape and after the release, with the focus where it was. Focus that can be seen: the same walk presses Tab six times from the search box and reads an outline on each control the keyboard lands on, which are the status filter, the type filter, a row, and the open document's header and two of its controls. A second check reads an outline on the collection's header, on its "table", "cards" and fold controls and on the search box.
- [x] Apply the design's title hierarchy, flat readable text, quiet materials and absence of blur over the moving field. Shown by the `glass-style` suite's test "no rule blurs anything, and no rule sets a backdrop filter", by the smoke run's check that a collection turned away from is dimmed under a veil of 0.48 "while it stays opaque", and by the header order in the walk's pictures: the view's name, then its count. Whether the result reads well is a person's judgement and belongs to [[TST-0063-A-Collection-And-Full-Note-Share-Glass]].

The collection is carried and dimmed with the desk when the field turns (the smoke run's `focus` check "the collection is carried and dimmed with it"), and it is drawn wholly inside the field (the `collection` suite's test "a field smaller than the collection draws it inside the field and changes nothing stored").

The `glass-collection` walk was added in commit `7103e3c`, with 11 checks, for the four boxes that were still open. It makes 17 since the review.

## Notes

The fixed navigator is replaced as the Glass list placement, not as the source of list behavior.

**Every box is ticked on a scripted check.** Four boxes were open after the first close-out because no check showed them. The `glass-collection` walk, written on 2026-10-02, drives each one. What a script cannot show stays with [[TST-0063-A-Collection-And-Full-Note-Share-Glass]], which has not been walked: whether the list reads well, and what a screen reader says.

**Where the evidence is from.** Every smoke check and walk cited above ran on 2026-10-02 at commit `18f5405`, in the Linux container (the `project-os-deck-smoke` image, Electron under Xvfb), after the independent review's fixes. The smoke run's `collection` part held 17 checks of 17. [[TST-0068-The-Glass-Desktop-Is-Walked-With-A-Real-Pointer]] held 55 of 55 in its first script, `glass-desktop`, and 17 of 17 in its second, `glass-collection`, with no part left out. The node suites passed 645 of 645 the same day at the same commit.

**What the independent review found in this task's boxes, 2026-10-02.** Each is fixed in the commit named. No tick was changed. The third and sixth boxes were rewritten on 2026-10-02 to cite the checks as they ran in the pass at `18f5405`, after the fixes.

- The sixth box, Escape. The walk's check that the box cited until the review could not fail on the defect both reviewers found. Escape put the collection back and left the drag's listeners attached, so the next move of a hand still on the button began the drag again and the release stored it. The walk let go without moving. Escape now ends the drag, and what the pointer does afterwards moves nothing and stores nothing (commit `716ae82`). The same commit handles Escape while the collection is resized by its corner. That key had reached Glass's own Escape, which leaves the focus or closes every note. The walk's Escape step now moves the pointer on before letting go, and a new step does the same for the corner; both held in the pass at `18f5405`. Two tests of the `collection` suite drive the built collection on a stand-in page for both, and round two of the review put each defect back and saw one test fail. Nobody has run the walk with the defect put back.
- The sixth box, focus that can be seen. The box said the walk does not read the outline on the search box, on the collection's header or on its fold control. Since commit `e3f1460` the walk has a check that does, and one on the served page in a window 760 px wide. Both held, and the box now cites the first.
- The third box, the served page. A collection the Mac had collapsed could not be opened there: the fold control, Enter and a double-click did nothing, and the list is that page's only way to a note with no card. On a page that cannot arrange the desk the fold is now that page's own. It tells the store nothing, and it stands until the Mac folds or opens the list (commit `bad5a5c`). The header's label for a screen reader names only the keys that work where it is drawn: Enter alone on the served page, and none in a narrow field.

The rule each fix settled is in [[FEAT-0020-Collections-And-Full-Notes-Live-On-Glass]], decisions 6 to 8 under "Settled while fixing what the review found". One thing is kept as it was: Escape with the header pressed and not yet dragged 5 px is not used by the collection, and goes on to Glass.

**Four fixes made after the first build belong here.**

- A key pressed in the collection no longer also turns the field (commit `2afecf1`). Right on a row slid the list 69 px, and End put it out of sight. The `keys` check on Left, Right, End and Home and the `collection` check "the field is not turned by them" would show the list moved, or the field's bearing changed, if it came back.
- Rows hold still under the pointer when a note is opened from the list (commit `d91fc7e`). A second press landed on the wrong row because the first note's rows arrived above it. The smoke run's `document` check "a second press 95 ms after the first landed on the row it was aimed at" covers it.
- A press on a row is not lost when the list is drawn again under it (commit `cee5119`). The row was handed to another element between the button going down and coming up, so the click went to the list and nothing opened. The `document` check "a press on FEAT-0011's row, held while FEAT-0008 was opened and the list drawn again, still opens FEAT-0011" asserts that the row was handed from one element to another in between, so it cannot pass without the redraw having happened.
- The list stays where it was when the collection is collapsed and opened again with a note open (commit `7103e3c`). The `glass-collection` walk found it: the script scrolls the list 60 px down, and the commit records that it came back at 665. The rows under "Joined to what you are holding" are ordered by where the cards stand round the open note, and that is known only a moment after the note opens. The rows were sorted afresh at every redraw, so the first redraw after the seats were known moved every row, and the list followed the row it remembered. The order is now worked out when what it describes changes (which notes are held, which is the focus) and once more when the seats round the focus are first known. The list's place is also restored only once the list is on screen again and the remembered row is there. The walk's check on the collapsed header reads the scroll position before and after, and fails if they differ.
