# Preview, apply and undo Glass arrangements

## Definition of Done

Each ticked box ends with what shows it. "The walk" is the scripted walk `glass-arrangements` ([[TST-0071-Collection-Forms-And-Arrangements-Are-Walked-With-A-Real-Pointer]]), which held 59 of 59 checks in the Linux box on 2026-10-02 at `18f5405`. "The suite" is `arrange` ([[TST-0070-An-Arrangement-Is-A-Plan-Before-It-Is-A-Move]]), 27 tests, part of the 645 of 645 that `npm test` passed at `18f5405`. "The smoke run" is the `arrange` part of the smoke run's Glass section: 4 checks, all passed, in the pass's loopback run at `18f5405` with each check printed (389 passed, 0 failed in the whole run).

- [x] Read, Compare and Show related each show a preview that names the objects that will move, with Apply and Cancel the keyboard reaches. Shown by the walk's checks "Read is shown before it is applied: an outline where the document will stand, the objects that move named, and the keyboard on Apply", "Compare names the two notes it is about and what it moves", "Tab goes from Apply to Cancel; Enter on Apply applies it" and "by keyboard: Read shown, Escape withdraws it and the keyboard is back on Read". All three commands were applied with Enter on Apply. Compare and Show related are also reached from the keyboard: "Tab goes from Read to Compare and to Show related, and each shows where the keyboard is; Enter on each shows what it would do, or says why it cannot, and Escape withdraws it with nothing moved and the keyboard back on the control". Where the walk applies those two it starts them with the pointer; Read alone is shown, applied and undone from the keyboard.
- [x] Compare keeps two full documents readable, preserves dimensions and reading positions, and has an explicit route back to the previous arrangement. Shown by the walk's checks "both documents keep their own, different sizes" (560 by 520 and 512 by 552), "and their own reading positions and text size" (scrolled to 220 and 140, text 14 px), "a link inside a compared document opens the note it names as a third document" (it opened PHASE-0001), "Undo arrangement puts both documents and the collection back where they were, at the sizes they had" and "it asks nothing, because nothing it moved had changed; the note opened since is still open and was not moved".
- [x] Apply never shrinks chosen text or pane size; narrow windows use navigation or the wider workspace and keep the collection reachable. Shown by the suite (a plan holds a place for each document and no size; the store's `arrange` action changes no size and no reading-size preference; in a window too narrow for both, Compare keeps both sizes and says by how many pixels they overlap) and by the walk's checks "applied, the document is the size it was, scrolled where it was, with text the size it was", "in a window too narrow for two documents side by side, the preview says by how many pixels they will overlap, they overlap by that much at their own sizes, and pressing the one behind brings it to the front" and "in a narrow window each of the two compared notes is reached by name, one at a time, with text the same size, and the sizes kept for them are untouched". In a window 1060 px wide the preview said "overlap by 164 px" and the two documents, 560 and 512 px wide, overlapped by 164. The bar in the narrow window offered Collection, FEAT-0002 and TASK-0080, and the walk pressed FEAT-0002. Pressing Collection there is the smoke run's check, in its `collection` part: ""Collection" on that bar puts the list in front at the field's width with the same exact count".
- [x] Undo arrangement puts back the layout and nothing else, leaves a note edit intact, and names what a person has moved, resized or closed since before it does anything. This box first asked for the undo to be "invalidated with explanation" after a manual move. The build does not throw the undo away: it names each changed object, offers "Undo the rest" and "Keep as it is", and puts back the others only on that second press (commit `b017807`). Shown by the smoke run's check "Apply moves what the preview showed … Undo arrangement, pressed, puts the store's desk and the collection's place back to the same bytes … and asks nothing, because nothing had been moved since", by the suite's tests 'an undo puts back exactly what the arrangement moved when nothing has changed since' and 'an undo names what a person changed since and leaves those objects alone', and by the walk's checks "an undo after TASK-0008 was moved by hand names it before doing anything, and offers to put back the rest or keep everything", "Undo the rest puts the other document back and leaves the moved one exactly where the person put it", "an undo after TASK-0008 was closed says so, and does not open it again" and "after an arrangement and its undo the tick is still in the file: the undo put back a layout, and nothing else". Three things the review found here are fixed, and each is shown. In a window shorter than the collection is stored, the walk's check held: the collection was stored 693 px high and drawn 423, Apply stored its new place and `h: 693`, and Undo arrangement asked nothing and left what is stored the same bytes. A note opened after the arrangement keeps its place in the stack, by the suite's test 'Undo leaves a note opened after the arrangement where it is in the stack: opened on top, it stays on top'. And when more has changed by the time "Undo the rest" is pressed, nothing is put back: the walk's check "FEAT-0002 was moved while the undo's question was on screen: "Undo the rest", pressed, puts nothing back, shows the question again with FEAT-0002 in its list, and says so in the status line".
- [x] Undo arrangement puts back the relationship that was picked out when the arrangement was applied. This box was split from the one above, which first said "restores only layout and emphasis". Shown by the walk's check "Undo arrangement puts back the relationship that was picked out when the arrangement was applied: "covers" is picked out again after it had been cleared". The walk picked out "covers" for FEAT-0002, applied Read, cleared the pick, and pressed Undo arrangement. The pick was back, the list was open, the same 5 rows were undimmed, and the list said ""covers": 5 of 28 picked out. All 28 are still listed."
- [x] A note that changes on disk while a preview is shown makes the application work the preview out again and say what changed before Apply, and Apply applies the plan on screen. This box first asked for the preview to be invalidated and recomputed "from the accepted current result rather than a copied member list". The build works it out again without being asked, which DES-0003, "Arrangement commands", allows ("invalidates or refreshes that preview visibly before Apply"). A plan holds the places of documents and of the collection and no list of members, so it has no copied member list. Shown by the suite's tests 'the basis of a plan changes when anything it was worked out from changes', which includes a changed result waiting, and 'a preview that was worked out again says what changed under it, and says nothing when nothing did'. Shown on screen by the walk's checks "a note changed on disk while Compare was shown: the preview was worked out again from what is there now and says that notes changed on disk, not that the desk did; the status line says the same sentence, and the preview's text is a polite live region", "TASK-0006 was opened on top of the two while Compare was shown: the preview is still about FEAT-0002 and TASK-0008, names TASK-0006 as what was opened in the preview and in the status line, and now says the other open note stays where it is" and "Apply then applies the plan on screen, not the one it replaced: FEAT-0002 and TASK-0008 are brought above TASK-0006, which the first plan, made when the two were already on top, would have left over them". The preview read "While this was shown, notes changed on disk, so it was worked out again from what is there now." Not shown: an Apply pressed at the instant the plan changes, which shows the new plan and applies nothing (commit `972ce73`). No test and no walk check drives it.
- [x] Relation emphasis uses supported types, reports the emphasized subset and retains the complete accessible neighbour list with one card per shared identity. Shown by the walk's checks "the relationships offered are exactly the frontmatter keys the files join it by, each with its count; a plain link in the text is not offered as a meaning" (seven keys for FEAT-0002), "picking out "covers" leaves exactly the 5 notes the files join by that key undimmed, says "5 of 28", and every row is still in the list", "the cards round it that are not part of it are dimmed, and none is removed", "a note joined only by a link in the text is given no meaning: it is dimmed under every pick", "clear shows every related note the same again, and no link was added or removed" and "a note joined to it is one card, however many ways it is joined". The count that is announced is for the document the chip is on: the walk's check ""covers" pressed in the list of FEAT-0002 while FEAT-0002 is not the focus says how many of the notes joined to FEAT-0002 it picks out: 5, the number its list picks out". The chip the Tab key arrived on showed a solid outline 2 px wide in the accent colour.
- [x] Reduced motion applies directly, local Escape cancels preview without sweeping the desk, and pure geometry/state plus real-pointer checks exercise these transitions. Shown by the walk's checks "Tab goes from Apply to Cancel; Enter on Apply applies it; with reduced motion the document is at its new place at once, with no travel" (with `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` emulated) and "Escape withdraws the preview and nothing else: nothing moved, the note is still open and still the focus, and the keyboard is back on Read", by the smoke run's check "Read, pressed, is shown before anything moves …, and Escape withdraws it and does nothing else: the store's desk and the collection's place are byte for byte what they were", and by the suite, which checks the plans and the undo without a window.
