# Collections and documents can be arranged and restored

## Goal

A person can turn a live collection into a compact stack, a readable table or an arrangement of its cards. Read, Compare and Show related make an occupied desk useful without manually moving every object.

## Scope

The feature follows FEAT-0020's collection and full-document interaction. It operates on the current view's collection and current desk. It adds presentation changes, deliberate arrangement commands, a preview, a labelled undo, and relationship emphasis over the existing source-backed connections.

- **Stack** is the collection collapsed to its header: the query's name, its exact count and what narrows it. Opening it shows the table or the cards, whichever it was.
- **Table** supplies the complete accessible rows and comes back to the row it was scrolled to.
- **Cards** draws those same members as cards inside the collection. It does not promise every card fits on screen: only the rows in view are drawn, a line under them says which of how many, and the table stays available. Drawing is limited to what is in view; membership is not.
- **Read** stands the document on top beside the list. **Compare** stands the two documents on top side by side, each at its own size. **Show related** gathers what the document on top is joined to and opens its complete list. The three commands take no selection: they are about the documents on top.
- A preview names the objects that will move and outlines where each will stand. When something changes under it, it is worked out again and says what changed. Apply commits the layout that is shown. Cancel leaves it unchanged. A labelled Undo arrangement puts back what the last one moved, independently of text editing and project actions.
- "Pick out", in a document's list of related notes, offers the frontmatter keys the files join the note by, each with its count. Picking one dims the cards, lines and rows that are not part of it, says how many of how many are picked out, and keeps every row. A plain link in the text is offered no meaning, and picking out changes no link.

Multiple independent collections, persistent arrangement presets, named scenes and camera persistence are outside this feature. Existing per-view desk storage holds the applied layout; preview and the last undo are per-window session state. FEAT-0023 owns named restoration and richer handoffs. Structured evidence objects belong to FEAT-0021 in Parity.

## Acceptance

- Stack, table and cards identify exactly the same current member set and count. Presentation changes preserve filters, selected identity and the table's scroll anchor.
- A note shared by a collection, an open document and multiple relationships has one spatial object on the desk. A table reference can remain without becoming a duplicate card.
- Read, Compare and Show related have keyboard-operable previews, apply and cancel. Compare displays two full documents at their chosen reading size.
- Applying an arrangement preserves text size, current reading position and chosen document dimensions. A narrow viewport keeps objects reachable through navigation or the wider workspace; it never shrinks text to fit.
- Undo restores the previous arrangement without undoing note edits or project actions. Stale previews or undo records announce incompatible changed objects before applying anything.
- Relationship emphasis names its subset while the exact full neighbour list remains available. Clearing it restores the previous emphasis without changing edges or membership.
- High populations remain entirely reachable with bounded visible drawing and motion. Reduced motion, scroll boundaries, visible keyboard focus and read-only tablet behavior match FEAT-0020.

## Decisions

Routine details settled while building, under DES-0003 as the baseline (REQ-0003, Approval). The reason is given where a commit or the code states one. Decisions 1 to 22 were settled while building, and each says what is true now. Decisions 23 to 35 were settled on 2026-10-02 by the review and its fixes.

1. **The stack is the collection collapsed to its header, not a third form.** The store keeps two values, `collapsed` and a form that is `table` or `cards`, so a collapsed collection still knows which form it opens as (`desktop/src/shared/collection.ts`).
2. **As cards, the members are the field's own cards moved into the collection.** No note is drawn twice. A member that is open as a document, or gathered round one, is a reference in its cell that finds the real one when pressed (commit `b017807`).
3. **Only the rows of cards in view are drawn.** The wheel, Page Down and End reach the rest, and a line under the cards says which are drawn of how many. Every member has an index, so all are reached by moving the first row, and that is what bounds the drawing (`cardGrid` in `collection.ts`).
4. **The cards open at the note the table was scrolled to, and the table comes back to that row.** The row is remembered by the note it is for, not by a number of pixels.
5. **The commands take no selection.** DES-0003 speaks of the "selected" document. In the build Read and Show related are about the document on top and Compare about the top two; pressing a document brings it to the top. With more than two open, the preview says the others stay where they are. The commit does not say why. The collection does mark one note as selected: the note last opened, on its row in the table and on its card. A change of form does not touch that mark, and no command reads it.
6. **A plan holds places and never a size.** It names a place for each document it moves, so nothing in a plan could change a size (`desktop/src/shared/arrange.ts`). Since the review this is true of the collection too (decision 24).
7. **What does not fit is said, and nothing is shrunk.** When the list and a document do not fit side by side, the plan collapses the collection to its header and says so. When two compared documents are wider than the field, they stand at its two sides and overlap, and the preview says by how many pixels and how the one underneath is reached (decision 33).
8. **A document the plan does not name stays where a person put it.** DES-0003, "Arrangement commands", asks for this.
9. **Read and Compare send gathered cards back to the field.** Reading and comparing are not gathering (a comment in `applyArrange`, `desktop/src/renderer/glass.ts`). Show related makes its document the focus and opens its list.
10. **Show related stands the document one row of cards down from the top, centred in the room beside the list.** The reason given in `arrange.ts` is that cards then have room on every side.
11. **A preview that goes stale is worked out again, not refused.** When the desk, the window, the notes or a note's links change while it is shown, the application works the preview out again and says what changed (decision 28). A preview about a note that has been closed is withdrawn. DES-0003 allows either invalidating or refreshing; the plan of 2026-10-01 said invalidate.
12. **An undo is kept for what has not changed.** When a person has moved, resized or closed something since, the undo names it and offers "Undo the rest" and "Keep as it is". The plan of 2026-10-01 said such a change invalidates the undo. The commit does not say why this was chosen. What happens when more changes while that question is on screen is decision 32.
13. **An undo puts back places, stacking, the collection's place and form, the focus, the open list and the picked-out relationship.** It does not put back a size, because no arrangement changes one; a document resized since is named and left alone. How the stacking goes back is decision 27.
14. **One undo, kept by the window.** It is gone after a reload, and it is dropped when a scene replaces the desk as a whole (`forgetArrangement`, FEAT-0023). It belongs to one workspace and one view (decision 31).
15. **A plan's places are whole pixels.** The store keeps whole pixels, so a plan rounds each place it names. A field 744.5 px high once gave a planned collection height of 720.5, the store kept 721, and the undo took the collection for one a person had resized. Since the review a plan writes no height for the collection at all (decision 24).
16. **The store applies an arrangement as one change.** The action is `arrange`, and it makes one revision for the documents, the stacking and the collection together.
17. **A command that cannot run is not a disabled button.** It is marked `aria-disabled` and says why when pressed, because a disabled button cannot be reached to learn why (a comment in `drawArrangeControls`).
18. **The relationships offered are frontmatter keys from Deck's own index of the files.** Each is offered with its count. "link" is never offered, so a plain link in the text is given no meaning.
19. **Under reduced motion an arrangement is a cut.** The documents and the collection are at their new places at once.
20. **The served page offers no arrangement and no change of form.** It draws the collection where the application put it.
21. **The field's bar no longer sets the width of the field area.** Once the bar gained the arrangement buttons, its width had pushed the area under the rail in a narrow window (commit `b017807`).
22. **Which row of the bar the arrangement buttons are on depends only on the window's width.** They are in the bar's group for what is on the desk. From 1500 px the bar is one row. Under 1500 px that group has a row of its own, and under 1200 px each of the bar's three groups has. The bar's height follows from the width alone, so a button appearing does not resize the field. Before this the bar wrapped wherever a row was full, and in a window 900 px wide the row holding Read, Compare and Show related was cut off by the bar's lower edge. Under about 760 px a row is narrower than its own buttons and scrolls sideways (commit `b085ad7`).
23. **Clearing "pick out" returns to nothing picked out, not to an earlier pick.** Decided 2026-10-02, and kept as built. The sixth acceptance line says clearing "restores the previous emphasis", and the review asked which state that means. One relationship is picked out at a time, and picking a second replaces the first. `clear` shows every related note the same again, so after picking one key and then another it does not go back to the first. The build reads "previous" as the even look before anything was picked out. Undo arrangement is the one thing that puts an earlier pick back: the one that stood when the arrangement was applied (decision 13).
24. **A plan is made from the collection as the store holds it, and never writes its width or height.** 2026-10-02, commit `b3c4219`. A plan changes where the collection stands and whether it is folded to its header. Where a document goes beside it, and the outline the preview draws, are worked out from that layout fitted to the field. Before this a plan was made from the collection as it is drawn. In a window shorter than the collection's stored height, Apply wrote the fitted height into the store, and Undo put the fitted layout back as if it were the one before.
25. **A view with nothing stored for its collection is planned from the default layout.** 2026-10-02, commit `b3c4219`. The default is what the collection is drawn from when the store holds none. When a plan moves or folds such a collection, after Apply and then Undo arrangement the store holds that default explicitly, where before the arrangement it held nothing. The default is as tall as the field allows, so what is stored is the size the window gave it at that moment. In a window of that size the collection is drawn where it was. Round two of the review noted this, and it is kept: the `arrange` action can store a layout for the collection or leave what is stored, and cannot take a stored layout away.
26. **The collection is named in a plan, and stored, only when it would be drawn somewhere else or in another form.** 2026-10-02, commit `b3c4219`. A collection taller than the field is drawn at the top whatever height down it is stored at. A plan that said it moves when nothing on screen would is not a preview of anything (a comment in `finish`, `arrange.ts`).
27. **An undo leaves a note opened since the arrangement where it is in the stack.** 2026-10-02, commit `d9dbb73`. The documents that were open when the arrangement was applied go back into the order they had, in the places in the stack they hold between them now. This reads "the documents the arrangement named" as every document that was open then. A note opened on top since stays on top. Before this the undo raised each of the earlier documents above it, so the note opened last ended at the bottom and nothing said so.
28. **A preview that was worked out again says what changed, compared with the preview as it was first shown.** 2026-10-02, commit `cc3c926`. The sentence begins "While this was shown," and names what it can know: which documents were opened, closed, moved, resized or brought to the front, what was done to the collection, that the window changed size, that notes changed on disk, that a note's links were read. Which note changed on disk is not named, because the field holds only how many changed notes are waiting. Comparing with the first preview means the sentence lists everything that has changed under it, not only the last change. The field says the sentence in its status line as well, and the preview's text is a polite live region (a part of the page a screen reader reads out when it changes). Before this the preview said "The desk changed while this was shown" whatever had changed, and nothing announced it.
29. **Apply applies only a plan that has been shown.** 2026-10-02, commit `972ce73`. Apply works the preview out once more from the desk at that instant. When that changes the plan, the press shows and says the new plan and applies nothing, and the next press applies it. Before this the new plan was applied in the same press, without having been shown.
30. **Show related says how many notes gather and how many its list holds, and gives both when they differ.** 2026-10-02, commit `cc3c926`. A neighbour that is open as a document has a row in the list and does not gather. While the note's links have not been read the preview says they are still being read, gives no number, and is worked out again when they arrive.
31. **An undo record belongs to one workspace and one view.** 2026-10-02, commit `fdb7e3e`. It is offered on that view of that workspace. On another view of the same workspace it is kept and not offered, and it is offered again when its view is back. When another workspace is opened it is forgotten, and it is not there when its own workspace is opened again. Two workspaces have views with the same id, and a record kept by view id alone was offered on a desk it had never arranged.
32. **"Undo the rest" answers the question that was shown.** 2026-10-02, commit `fdb7e3e`. The press works the desk out again. The question is the same one when the list of what has changed is the same and the same objects would go back. When it is not, the question is shown again with the new list, the status line says nothing was put back, and that press puts nothing back. A note opened in between is not something the arrangement moved, so it does not change the question. When everything that had changed is back where the arrangement left it, the question offers "Undo arrangement" for all of it.
33. **Compare says how the note underneath is reached, as the build offers it.** 2026-10-02, commit `fdb7e3e`. Where the two overlap in part, "pressing either brings it to the front". Where the note on top covers the other completely there is nothing of the other to press, and the preview says "B covers A completely. Tab reaches A's header, and Enter there brings it to the front." In a narrow field, which shows one object at a time, it says "the bar along its top names A and B, and pressing a name shows that note." The field tells the plan whether it shows one object at a time, and that is part of what makes a preview stale.
34. **"Pick out" counts for the document its chip is on.** 2026-10-02, commit `fdb7e3e`. The sentence names that document, and says "1 of the notes … is picked out" for one. Before this the count was taken through the document in focus, so a chip pressed on any other document said "0 of the notes" while its list picked out the right rows.
35. **The "pick out" chips and the references among the cards show keyboard focus as an outline.** 2026-10-02, commit `1603042`. It is the outline every other control in `deck.css` has, 2 px in the accent colour, and the hover border stays. A pressed chip has the accent border already, and a reference's border is one dashed pixel, so the border alone did not show where the keyboard was.

## Impact analysis

Checked REQ-0001/0002 and FEAT-0020 for exact counts, chosen size, immediate reading and input. REQ-0003 adds forms and arrangements without relaxing those constraints. FEAT-0015 keeps one desk per view and FEAT-0016 keeps wheel input spatial outside objects. FEAT-0017's old single-focus dock rule is superseded for Glass by FEAT-0020's multiple readable documents. Compare builds on that capability. TASK-0102 owns the arrangement preview, application and undo; TASK-0104 supplies the identity and geometry prerequisite.

Applying layout is explicit. It does not reclassify a note's priority, modify its status or change the band's source rules in FEAT-0018. Exact list access remains distinct from ISS-0086's placement issue. No native migration, cockpit contract or new write authority is required. Camera persistence remains outside scope, so the current session-state contract stands.
