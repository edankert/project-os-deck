# Save, reopen and undo scenes in Glass

Glass has the controls for scenes: a list on the bar above the field, with "open", "save scene", "rename", "delete" and an undo back to the desk that was there. This task draws what TASK-0106 modelled and adds the two things only a window knows: where each document is being read, and how large the field is. It was built in commit `b1bfa1d` and changed in `9d94fa0` and `40a74e3`. The bar the controls stand on was rebuilt in `3ff281c` and `b085ad7`, after a scripted walk twice found a control cut off in a narrow window. Commit `972985f` fixed three things the second close-out of these notes reported, and the commits merged in `71522c6` fixed what the independent review found.

**Where it stands, 2026-10-02.** The task is `done`. Every box under the Definition of Done is ticked and every step is taken. The last box, a visible focus on the scene controls and the two buttons of the scene's message by keyboard, was open until the last pass: commit `972985f` added checks for it to the walk, and they held. The evidence is the scripted walk `glass-scenes`, run in a Linux container on 2026-10-02 at commit `18f5405`, on a copy of this repository and on a copy of `your-trainer`. It makes 43 checks, and all held on both ([[TST-0076-Scenes-And-Handoff-Are-Walked-With-A-Real-Pointer]]). A quoted check below is one of that walk's, as it read on this repository. What is still a person's is the acceptance walk, [[TST-0073-A-Scene-Reopens-And-A-Note-Crosses-Screens]].

**After the independent review, 2026-10-02.** The reviewers ran no window, so they checked this task by reading its code and by probing the store under it. What they found in this task's own code: a scene with no document that had text reopened with no message at all; a note arriving during a reopen swallowed the scene's report; "save scene" and "rename" took a name the address refuses; Spread's "save desk" replaced a scene without asking; the controls offered "rename" for an entry this Deck cannot read; "back to the desk before" did not put back what a scene replaced on another view; and the served page, sent an address that names a scene, scrolled documents and said the scene had reopened. Each is fixed (`22678b9`, `509c770`, `ff25f28`, `093c198`, `2646cf5`), and [[FEAT-0023-A-Glass-Scene-Reopens-And-Crosses-Screens]] has each finding under Review. The scenes walk has a check for five of them, four new and one rewritten: the other view's desk and list put back by the undo, a scene with no note open, "save desk" under a scene's name and under an unreadable one's, what is offered for an unreadable entry, and the served page sent such an address. What "save scene" and "rename" say of a refused name is in the renderer and is not walked. All five checks held in the pass at `18f5405`, on both workspaces, and each is quoted in the box it belongs to below. A second round of the review, at `cbae0d3`, found the refuted claims fixed; it ran no window either.

## Notes

**The bar in a narrow window.** The bar keeps each group of controls on a row fixed by the window's width: one row from 1500 pixels, a row of its own for what is on the desk under 1500, and a row for each of its three groups under 1200 (commit `b085ad7`). Its height depends only on the window's width. The scenes walk found why that was needed, twice. At `aa848b0`, in a window 900 pixels wide, the bar's third row was cut off, and the walk's press on "close all" landed on the collection's header behind it. That raised the collection over both documents, and the check of their headers failed. Commit `3ff281c` gave the bar a third row and made a walk's press stop when the control meant is not what is drawn at the point. At `3ff281c` the walk on a copy of `your-trainer` stopped at the same press: that workspace's bar held a longer line and needed a fourth row. From `e86b2e4` on the walk holds on both workspaces, and it held again at `18f5405`. Its pictures at 1180 and at 900 pixels show three rows, each whole. At 1440 pixels the bar is two rows and both undo controls are whole.

**The scene's message lay over documents, and was moved.** In the pictures of the pass at `e86b2e4` the message covered the right half of both documents' headers (`03-reopened-after-changes.png`), the right end of one (`04-deleted-with-restore.png`) and the right half of the front one (`09-a-scene-in-a-smaller-window.png`). The buttons at the right end of a header were under it until it was dismissed. Commit `972985f` moved it to the field's lower left, above the compass's line. The walk's check in the smaller window requires both ends of each header to be clear while the message is shown, and it held. Two pictures of the pass at `18f5405` were looked at for this close-out. In `03-reopened-after-changes.png` the message lies over the lower part of the collection and over the left edge of the front document's text, and both headers are clear. In `09-a-scene-in-a-smaller-window.png`, a window 900 pixels wide, it lies over several lines of the front document's text and over rows of the collection. The text under it scrolls, and the message goes when it is dismissed. Nobody has judged whether that place is the right one.

**A line of Glass's own lies over a header in one picture.** In `09-a-scene-in-a-smaller-window.png` of the pass at `18f5405`, a line reading "the desk is swept; every note is back in its slot" stands over the middle of the front document's header, on its title. It is what Glass says after "close all", which the walk had just pressed, and it is not the scene's message. The check looks at the two ends of the header and not at its middle. How long that line stays was not looked at.

Spread's existing "Save desk…" control keeps working and saves through the same store actions. Since `ff25f28` it asks before it replaces a scene this Deck can read, and is refused with the reason over an entry this Deck cannot read. Whether Spread should list scenes differently is not part of this task. One consequence is left open and is in the feature note's Review section: Spread's list of saved desks opens a scene straight through the store, with no "back to the desk before", no document put back where it was read and no message. Choosing an unreadable entry there opens nothing and says why (`972ce73`).

**A reopened scene always gets its message.** A scene with no document to put back is answered at once and says it reopened. A note that arrives from another window while a scene is still waiting for its documents no longer swallows the scene's report (commit `22678b9`). The waiting is kept by `ReadingWait` in `desktop/src/shared/scenes.ts`, which a node suite tests; that `glass.ts` calls it is held by the walk's check of a scene saved with no note open.

**The served page and an address that names a scene.** The page opens none, scrolls nothing and says that the address names a saved desk and that it shows the desk the Mac has (commits `2646cf5`, `4f5d266`).

Opening a scene narrows every window on the workspace, because the search text and filters are shared. The message a person sees in a second window when that happens is not designed here, and no walk looked at a second window while a scene was opened.
