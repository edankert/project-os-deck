# Save, reopen and undo scenes in Glass

Glass has the controls for scenes: a list on the bar above the field, with "open", "save scene", "rename", "delete" and an undo back to the desk that was there. This task draws what TASK-0106 modelled and adds the two things only a window knows: where each document is being read, and how large the field is. It was built in commit `b1bfa1d` and changed in `9d94fa0` and `40a74e3`. The bar the controls stand on was rebuilt in `3ff281c` and `b085ad7`, after a scripted walk twice found a control cut off in a narrow window.

**Where it stands, 2026-10-02.** The task stays `doing`. One box is open, and no check covers it: a visible focus on each scene control, and the two buttons of the scene's message by keyboard. Everything else is ticked. The evidence is the scripted walk `glass-scenes`, run in a Linux container at commit `e86b2e4` on a copy of this repository and on a copy of `your-trainer`. It makes 38 checks, and all held on both ([[TST-0076-Scenes-And-Handoff-Are-Walked-With-A-Real-Pointer]]). A quoted check below is one of that walk's, as it read on this repository.

## Notes

**The bar in a narrow window.** The bar keeps each group of controls on a row fixed by the window's width: one row from 1500 pixels, a row of its own for what is on the desk under 1500, and a row for each of its three groups under 1200 (commit `b085ad7`). Its height depends only on the window's width. The scenes walk found why that was needed, twice. At `aa848b0`, in a window 900 pixels wide, the bar's third row was cut off, and the walk's press on "close all" landed on the collection's header behind it. That raised the collection over both documents, and the check of their headers failed. Commit `3ff281c` gave the bar a third row and made a walk's press stop when the control meant is not what is drawn at the point. At `3ff281c` the walk on a copy of `your-trainer` stopped at the same press: that workspace's bar held a longer line and needed a fourth row. At `e86b2e4` the walk holds on both workspaces. Its pictures at 1180 and at 900 pixels show three rows, each whole. At 1440 pixels the bar is two rows and both undo controls are whole.

**The scene's message lies over documents.** In `03-reopened-after-changes.png` the message covers the right half of both documents' headers, in `04-deleted-with-restore.png` the right end of one, and in `09-a-scene-in-a-smaller-window.png` the right half of the front one. The buttons at the right end of a header are under it until it is dismissed. No check asserts where the message stands, and nobody has decided where it should.

Spread's existing "Save desk…" control keeps working and saves through the same store actions; whether Spread should list scenes differently is not part of this task.

Opening a scene narrows every window on the workspace, because the search text and filters are shared. The message a person sees in a second window when that happens is not designed here, and no walk looked at a second window while a scene was opened.
